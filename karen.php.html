About

Karen Marie Kasigila holds the pivotal role of Director of Data Science at Africa Climate Finance. As a Director, she sits at the executive table to ensure that every investment is backed by rigorous analytical modeling. She oversees the development of proprietary risk-assessment tools, tracks real-time carbon sequestration data, and leads the team in translating complex climate datasets into actionable financial strategies.

Karen is completing her Master’s in Data Analytics in October 2027, building on her foundation in data science, financial modeling, and climate finance strategy.

Expertise

  • Climate finance strategy and policy alignment with UN SDGs
  • Data science: Python, SQL, machine learning, predictive modeling
  • Financial modeling and data visualization for policy and lending
  • Founder & Developer: ImaraCare, AI-powered remote diagnostics platform

Leadership

PR Chair, Black Student Christian Forum; Student Mentor, Andrews University; Volunteer, Dar es Salaam Children’s Homes.
