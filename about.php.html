Who we are

Bridging grassroots communities and institutional capital.

Africa Climate Finance is a climate-finance organization bridging grassroots communities and institutional capital to turn climate ambitions into investable, lasting solutions.

We connect village-level microfinance, cooperatives and smallholder farmers with the financial structures, technical expertise and investment opportunities required to mobilize climate capital.

Our work spans

  • Financial literacy
  • Climate-smart lending
  • Agricultural value-chain development
  • Market analysis
  • Investment readiness
  • Project preparation

Working from communities such as Rungwe to national and international funding platforms, we ensure local climate priorities are translated into credible, fundable projects.

We believe climate action must be financially sustainable, inclusive and measurable—because climate solutions that cannot be financed cannot endure.

The model

Community education → microfinance → project preparation → climate funds

Mission

Fill the gap of climate finance in Africa via microfinance for private and vulnerable communities, aligned with low-carbon, climate-resilient pathways and SDG 13.

Pathway

Establish the Climate-Smart Microfinance Initiative in Tanzania, using Central Bank licensing to de-risk and scale climate investment, then replicate proven models more widely.
