Climate Finance Services

What is climate finance?
Climate finance refers to local, national or transnational financing that supports mitigation and adaptation actions to address climate change. It includes the Adaptation Fund and other multilateral and bilateral sources.
What services does Africa Climate Finance offer?
We offer market analysis, financial modelling, microfinance advisory, gender assessment, stakeholder engagement, scientific assessment, and climate risk analysis for projects and programmes advancing climate action.
How can I engage your services?
Please contact us via our contact page with your project or programme details. We will respond to discuss scope, timeline, and engagement terms.
Do you work outside Tanzania?
Yes. We work across East Africa and the broader African continent, supporting governments, development partners, and private sector clients with climate finance advisory and project development.

From Concept Note to Funding Proposal

Concept Notes (CNs) are early project summaries submitted to climate funders, outlining objectives, beneficiaries, and estimated impact.

Project Preparation Facilities (PPFs) provide funding to develop a full Funding Proposal, including feasibility studies, environmental and social safeguards, and stakeholder engagement.

Funding Proposals (FPs) are comprehensive applications for climate finance — including FP179 (US$200M total project value) and FP223 (US$1.5B total project value), supported by our leadership — a combined US$1.7B climate finance pipeline.
