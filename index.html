The imbalance

<4%

Africa contributes less than 4% of global CO₂ emissions.

In Tukuyu, one drought can still take a whole season. Communities that contribute least to the crisis live closest to its consequences.

Climate finance is not a slogan. It is the instrument that turns a failed harvest into a buffer, a local enterprise into a livelihood, and a project into something a funder can sign.

National plans still fall short of 1.5°C. The gap is capital, structure, and access.

Who we are

Bridging grassroots communities and institutional capital.

Africa Climate Finance is a climate-finance organization bridging grassroots communities and institutional capital to turn climate ambitions into investable, lasting solutions. We connect village-level microfinance, cooperatives and smallholder farmers with the financial structures, technical expertise and investment opportunities required to mobilize climate capital.

Working from communities such as Rungwe to national and international funding platforms, we ensure local climate priorities are translated into credible, fundable projects.

Capabilities

Seven services. One job: make climate capital usable.

We do not offer a catalogue of consulting products. These are the instruments we use to move a climate idea from a conversation to a financed activity.

  • 01 Market Analysis

    Demand studies, buyer mapping, and pricing analysis for bankable climate projects.
  • 02 Financial Modelling

    Feasibility models, cashflow projections, and investment-ready financial structures.
  • 03 Microfinance for Climate

    Central Bank of Tanzania-licensed climate-smart lending for cooperatives, SMEs, and vulnerable communities.
  • 04 Gender Assessment

    Gender action plans and women-led enterprise assessments for climate finance proposals.
  • 05 Stakeholders Engagement

    Stakeholder mapping, consultation, and FP documentation for climate projects.
  • 06 Scientific Assessment

    Climate science review, baseline data, and technical validation for proposals.
  • 07 Climate Risk

    Physical and transition risk analysis for portfolios and project pipelines.
