  • What We Do

Africa Climate Finance prepares, structures, and deploys climate finance. Some of this work happens in a classroom in Rungwe. Some of it happens in a financial model, a gender action plan, or a funding proposal. All seven services below are live capabilities.

  • 01 Market Analysis

    Demand studies, buyer mapping, and pricing analysis for bankable climate projects.
  • 02 Financial Modelling

    Feasibility models, cashflow projections, and investment-ready financial structures.
  • 03 Microfinance for Climate

    Central Bank of Tanzania-licensed climate-smart lending for cooperatives, SMEs, and vulnerable communities.
  • 04 Gender Assessment

    Gender action plans and women-led enterprise assessments for climate finance proposals.
  • 05 Stakeholders Engagement

    Stakeholder mapping, consultation, and FP documentation for climate projects.
  • 06 Scientific Assessment

    Climate science review, baseline data, and technical validation for proposals.
  • 07 Climate Risk

    Physical and transition risk analysis for portfolios and project pipelines.
